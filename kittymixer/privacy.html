Kitty Mixer – Privacy Policy

Developer: Techbizzy (techbizzy.com) · Contact: techbizzy.com/contact · Last updated: 8 November 2026

Summary

Kitty Mixer: Color Sort Puzzle ("the game") does not ask for your name, email or location and has no user accounts. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The game shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases through Google services, which process some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, coins, boosters, settings and purchase records are saved locally on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to show, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice anytime in the game under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can deliver it.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If leaderboards are enabled, your Google Play Games profile name and scores are shared with Google Play Games to show rankings.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we don't hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled through Google's controls above. For any question, contact us at techbizzy.com/contact.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
