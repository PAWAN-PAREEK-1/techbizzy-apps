Motorkin – Privacy Policy

Developer: Techbizzy (techbizzy.com) · Contact: techbizzy.com/contact · Last updated: 8 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a hashed password, if you create an account.
  • Vehicle data you enter (vehicles, services, fuel, costs, places). It stays on your phone unless you turn on cloud backup.
  • Location, only while you use the map, parking or trip features. Trips and GPS points stay on your phone.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads, with your consent where the law requires it (EU/UK consent form).
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. We never see your card details.

Service providers

MongoDB Atlas (database for cloud backup and accounts), Vercel (hosting), Google AdMob (ads), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Google Gmail (sign-up and password emails).

Your rights

You can export your data and delete your account at any time in the app under Me. See how to delete your account. Users in the EU/UK have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection under the GDPR; contact us through techbizzy.com/contact. You can change ad choices in the app under Me → Ad privacy choices.

Children

Motorkin is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
