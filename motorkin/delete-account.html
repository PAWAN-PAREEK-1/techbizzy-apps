Delete your Motorkin account

Motorkin is developed by Techbizzy.

In the app

  1. Open Motorkin and go to Me.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Send a deletion request through techbizzy.com/contact with the email address of your account. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account (email and password) and all cloud-backup data (vehicles, services, fuel, costs, places) are permanently deleted. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app. Purchase records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat follow their own retention rules.
